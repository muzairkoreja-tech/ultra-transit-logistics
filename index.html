Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which equipment types do you dispatch?

Box trucks, semi trucks (dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck), cargo vans, sprinter vans, car haulers and most other commercial equipment. Not sure? Just ask.

What does dispatch cost?

Box trucks 6%, semi trucks 5–6% and cargo/sprinter vans 8% of the gross load amount. Car haulers and specialized equipment are quoted individually. Your rate is confirmed in writing before you start.

How soon can I start getting loads?

Most carriers are fully set up within 24–72 hours of sending their documents. Your dispatcher starts looking for freight as soon as your packet is active.

Will I be forced to take loads?

No. You approve or decline every load. We bring you the best options we find — the final decision is always yours.

Are you a broker, load board or factoring company?

No. Ultra Transit Logistics is a dispatch and trucking services company. Load boards and factoring companies are independent third parties we use or introduce you to.

Can you help with an MC number, trailers or an ELD?

Yes. Beyond dispatch we offer MC leasing, trailer rental and leasing, ELD setup help, equipment sourcing, factoring connections, oversize load support and more — all from one team.
