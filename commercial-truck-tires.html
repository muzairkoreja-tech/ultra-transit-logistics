Commercial Tire FAQs

Which commercial tires do you supply?

Steer, drive and trailer tires for owner-operators, fleets and trucking companies — new and used, depending on current stock.

Which sizes can I get?

Common sizes include 295/75R22.5, 11R22.5 and 285/75R24.5. Need something else? Send us the size and we'll look for it.

Do you offer volume pricing?

Yes — fleets, tire shops and anyone buying multiple tires can ask for volume pricing.

Where do you deliver?

Pickup and delivery options depend on your location and order size. Larger orders can be shipped across the U.S. — tell us where you are and we'll work out the best option.
